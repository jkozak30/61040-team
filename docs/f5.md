## Updated Development Plan

Since F4, we styled the discussion board and AI overview, improved discussion navigation and playback following, and made the sidebar resizable with collapsible sections. We also integrated `Summarizing` with `Watching` to support a video overview and suggested answers. Discussion summaries now have the option to be "enhanced" by video content by additional request, which will provide a video summary and suggested question answers. Summarizing also checks whether the currently-stored summary is stale, and enables regenerating when this is the case.

Since improving `Discussing` and `Summarizing` was our main priority, we have deferred Panopto support beyond the final submission and are focusing on YouTube for this version. The remaining plan is largely the same, with emphasis on improving our current features and finalizing deliverables.
